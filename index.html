📐 Methodology

The Science Behind
Your Sustainability Score

AquaValue AI computes a 100-point Sustainability Score from 4 internationally benchmarked KPIs. Every formula and threshold references a real global standard.

35 pts
1

Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR)

Reference: ASC Shrimp Standard v1.1 · BAP Farm Standard Sec.6

Feed accounts for 60–70% of total production cost. Excess feeding is the primary cause of water body degradation and greenhouse gas emissions.

FCR = Total Feed Used (kg) ÷ Total Harvested Yield (kg)
  • ≤ 1.20 → 35 pts — Excellent
  • 1.21–1.40 → 25 pts — Industry Standard
  • 1.41–1.60 → 15 pts — Below Average
  • > 1.60 → 0 pts — High Waste
25 pts
2

Energy & Carbon Intensity

Reference: FAO GHG Emissions Guidelines · ISO 14064 Scope 2

Aerators and pump systems are the largest electricity consumers on a shrimp farm. Energy intensity per ton of output directly reflects carbon footprint.

Energy Intensity (kWh/Ton) = Total kWh ÷ Yield (Tons)
  • ≤ 2,500 kWh/Ton → 25 pts — High Efficiency
  • 2,501–3,500 → 15 pts — Farm Average
  • > 3,500 → 5 pts — Over-consuming
20 pts
3

Animal Welfare & Survival Rate

Reference: FAO Code of Conduct · BAP Animal Welfare Standard

High survival rates reflect excellent water quality management, good animal welfare conditions, and reduced antibiotic dependency—all critical for export certification.

Survival Rate (%) = (Harvested Count ÷ Stocked Post-Larvae) × 100
  • ≥ 80% → 20 pts — Excellent
  • 65%–79% → 12 pts — Acceptable
  • < 65% → 0 pts — Disease Risk
20 pts
4

Water Resource Management

Reference: Thailand GAP (TAS 7401) · ASC Effluent Standard 2.2

Controlling water exchange and preventing untreated effluent from entering public waterways is a core requirement of ASC and Thai GAP certification.

Water Exchange Ratio = Water Discharged (m³) ÷ Pond Volume (m³)
  • ≤ 1.0 → 20 pts — Closed System
  • 1.1–3.0 → 10 pts — Semi-closed
  • > 3.0 → 0 pts — Open Discharge
